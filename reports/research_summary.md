# Streaming habits in India: research summary
*Simulated study. Base: 452 valid completes (402 streaming users) after removing 48 records that failed quality checks.*

- **YouTube** is the most widely used platform across all three age bands.
- Netflix use falls from 55.7% of streamers aged 18-24 to 32.9% of those aged 35-45, a gap of 22.8 percentage points.
- Most-used platform by age band: 18-24: YouTube; 25-34: YouTube; 35-45: YouTube.
- Share willing to pay at least Rs 200 a month: 18-24: 18.6%; 25-34: 32.8%; 35-45: 46.3%.

*Independent simulation for portfolio purposes, not real survey data.*
